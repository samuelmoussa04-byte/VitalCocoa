## ⏳ Next Steps (Manual Configuration in Shopify Admin)

### Step 1: Remove Password Protection
**Goal:** Make store publicly accessible

1. Go to [Shopify Admin](https://admin.shopify.com/store/sjwjsx-uv)
2. Click **Online Store** → **Preferences**
3. Under "Store access," find the **Password** field
4. Click the field and **clear it completely** (delete "mowngi")
5. Click **Save**

✅ Your store will now be accessible at: `https://sjwjsx-uv.myshopify.com`

---

### Step 2: Set Up Markets (US & CA)
**Goal:** Enable automatic USD/CAD switching based on visitor location

1. Go to [Markets](https://admin.shopify.com/store/sjwjsx-uv/markets)
2. You'll see **Canada market** already created
3. Click **"Create United States Market"** button
4. Select **United States** and **USD** currency
5. Click **Create**

**Result:** 
- Visitors from USA see USD prices
- Visitors from Canada see CAD prices
- Header currency switcher lets customers manually switch

---

### Step 3: Create Your Product
**Goal:** Add VitalCocoa to your store

1. Go to **Products** → **Add Product**
2. Product name: `VitalCocoa` (or your preferred name)
3. Description: (Add your product description)
4. Add a placeholder image (temporarily; replace with actual images later)
5. **Variants** - Create 3 variants:
   - Variant 1: **Quantity: 1 Tub**
     - Price (USD): $39.99
     - Barcode: (optional)
   - Variant 2: **Quantity: 3 Tubs**
     - Price (USD): $99.99
   - Variant 3: **Quantity: 6 Tubs**
     - Price (USD): $179.99

6. **US Market Pricing** - Set prices for each variant (should auto-fill from above)
7. **CA Market Pricing** - Set CAD prices:
   - 1 Tub: $51.99 CAD
   - 3 Tubs: $129.99 CAD
   - 6 Tubs: $233.99 CAD
   (Adjust as needed based on your margin)

8. Click **Save**
